## Why

An external review (2026-10-04) pointed at T89: in zero setup, 10 of 12 honest runs that left the task unsolved were READY, because nothing that passed before broke. Readers take READY as "the work is done". Owner decision (2026-10-04): separate the two.

## What changed (contract 0046)

- **READY** needs at least one mandatory acceptance check in the contract, and every mandatory check passed.
- **NO REGRESSIONS** (`no-regressions`): every mandatory check passed, but the contract has no acceptance check (`ohx init --lock-tests`, `ohx gate` without a contract). Nothing that passed before broke; nothing shows the task is done.
- Both exit 0, so CI and the Claude Code Stop hook still let the work through. On NO REGRESSIONS the hook tells the user what it means and how many locked tests still fail.
- Every report has `claims` and a section "What this verdict supports": no regressions (yes, no, not known), acceptance criteria (met, not met, none), and the tests that still fail as they did before.

Existing tests changed on purpose: a plain gate, the Stop hook after a passing change and the cheat demo's genuine fix now show NO REGRESSIONS instead of READY.
